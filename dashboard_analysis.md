# Dashboard Analysis Report: BeanStream Analytics

Based on the data visualized in the BeanStream Analytics Dashboard, here is the analysis for the management team:

### 1. What happened to DAU, revenue, and margin?
*   **DAU (Daily Active Users):** Shows a healthy upward trend, growing from approximately 500 to over 1,000 users over the 120-day period.
*   **Revenue:** Follows the DAU growth, increasing steadily as more users are converted into customers.
*   **Margin:** Unlike the growth in users and revenue, the profit margin has experienced a significant downward trend, particularly in the latter half of the period.

### 2. When did the margin start to decline?
The margin started to decline significantly around **Day 60**. This coincides with a sharp increase in **Discount Amounts** and **Operational Costs** implemented in the simulation logic to reflect aggressive promotion and scaling challenges.

### 3. Which platform performs better overall?
**Android** performs better in terms of user volume. The data shows that Android consistently has a higher DAU (approximately 20% higher than iOS), which drives a larger portion of the total revenue, despite iOS users having a slightly higher Average Order Value (AOV).

### 4. Which branch is currently at risk based on the data?
The **Kemang** branch is currently at risk. While branches like *Central Park* and *Sudirman* are driving high profits, Kemang's performance is significantly lower (simulated at 70% of the base performance). With the declining overall margins, Kemang is likely the first branch to potentially face net losses.

### 5. What might happen if the current trend continues for another 60 days?
If the current trend continues:
*   **Profitability Crisis:** The overall profit margin could drop to near zero or become negative as operational costs and discounts outpace revenue growth.
*   **Cash Flow Issues:** Despite growing revenue, the actual cash kept (profit) will shrink, making it difficult to sustain the increased operational costs.
*   **Branch Closures:** Underperforming branches like Kemang might become unsustainable and require closure or significant restructuring.

### 6. What action would you recommend to the management team?
*   **Optimize Discount Strategy:** Gradually reduce the aggressive 15-20% discount rate seen after Day 60. Shift towards targeted loyalty rewards rather than blanket discounts.
*   **Control Operational Costs:** Investigate the cause of the 40% spike in operational costs and implement efficiency measures.
*   **Branch-Specific Strategy:** Conduct a deep dive into the Kemang branch to understand if the issue is location-specific or requires a change in local management/marketing.
*   **iOS Engagement:** Since iOS users have a higher AOV, implement marketing campaigns specifically targeted at iOS users to increase their share of the total user base.
